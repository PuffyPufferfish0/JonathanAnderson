**Current(I)**: transfer of positive charge per unit time
**I = dq/dt**
current is always defined <u>through</u> devices ( analogous to fluid flow)
current always has a magnitude in *amperes*, and a direction, labeled with an arrow
*IGNORE ELECTRONS IN THIS CLASS*

**Voltage(V)**: is necessary for current flow, and is called potential
analogous to pressure
voltage is always defined across devices, i.e. difference or drop
voltage has magnitude in volts
polarity with (+ or -)

**Power(P)**: P = VI
magnitude in watts(w) 
sign tells us whether power is being created or absorbed

*Sign convention*
1) voltage drops are positive with current flow (voltage rises are negative)
2) positive power → dissipation (absorbed)
	negative power → generation (created)
