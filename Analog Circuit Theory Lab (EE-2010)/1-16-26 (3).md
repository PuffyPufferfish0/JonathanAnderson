*Resistors*: a two terminal device that opposes the flow of current
unit: ohm (Ω)
*Ohms Law*
R = v/I or V= IR or I = v/R
resistors can only dissipate power!
passive device
therefore voltage always drops in the direction of positive current
power in a resistor: P = VI
therefore P = I * R * I = I^2 R
or P=V(v/r) = V^2/(R)
physical resistors have a resistance and a power rating 
small resistors used in electronics: 1/8 w, 1/4w, 1/2w, 1w, 2w
small carbon film resistors in lab: 1/4w 5% tolerance
significance of power rating is that resistors dissipate power by giving off heat
power rating is just as important as value
for long life and reliability, de-rate(use higher power rating than necessary)
factor of 3 is a good idea
