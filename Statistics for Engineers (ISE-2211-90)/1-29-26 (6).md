*Cumulative probabilities* 
alternative way of describing probabilities associated with a random variable
P(X< x)
up to and including some value of X, rather than the probability of an exact value

**sampling with replacement:** item is selected, placed back into the batch(randomly); next selection has the same probability distro as previous selection ... independent

**sampling without replacement** is when an item is selected not placed back in; probabilities associated with next selection depend on outcome of previous selection... not independent!!

ex: 850 manufactured parts, it is known that 50 contain flaws. assembly requires 2 parts selected at random without replacement. 
write a cumulative distro function for the number of non conforming parts in an assembly

P{ gb } = 800/850 x 50/849 = .05543 or 5.5%
