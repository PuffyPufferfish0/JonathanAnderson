*Sample Spaces*
**Random experiment:** same experiment repeated the same way every time gives <u>different</u> outcomes
**Sample Spaces**: set of all possible outcomes of a random experiment
denoted with an S{}
![[Pasted image 20260114075416.png]]
**discrete**: finite or "countably infinite" set of outcomes
S{yes no}
S{heads tails}
S{1 2 3}

**Continuous sample space:** interval of real numbers
S{ 118 <V<122}
**Event:** subset or included unit in a sample space, denoted E1 E2 E3
combination of existing events is also an event

*Set Operations*
<u>Union </u> of two events: includes all outcomes in either event 

E1∪E2

**intersection of two events:** includes only outcomes common to both events

E1 ∩ E2

**complement of an event:** all outcomes in sample space not in the event, including outcomes not in other events.

E'

![[Pasted image 20260114080353.png]]

Sample space is 
S{yy yn ny nn}

E1: outcomes in which at least one part conforms *{yy yn ny}*
E2: neither part conforms *{nn}*
E5: at least one fails to conform *{yn ny nn}*
![[Pasted image 20260114080654.png]]
E1∪E5 = {yy yn ny nn}=S (entire sample space)
E1 ∩ E5 = {yn ny}
E1' = {nn}
